# 🌲 KBR Park Weekend Trail

> **Summary:** A scenic 6km trail around KBR Park, featuring entry gates, water stations, and shaded canopy segments, suitable for both cycling and jogging.

- **Distance:** 6.0 km
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Est. Duration:** 120 mins
- **Elevation Gain:** +50m

---

## 📍 Waypoints

### 1. [START] Main Entrance
Start your journey at the main entrance of KBR Park.
*Coords: 37.7749, -122.4194*

### 2. [WATER_STOP] Water Station 1
Refill your water supply at the first water station.
*Coords: 37.7751, -122.4196*

### 3. [SCENIC_VIEW] Shaded Canopy Segment
Enjoy the shaded canopy as you cycle through this scenic segment.
*Coords: 37.7753, -122.4198*

### 4. [WATER_STOP] Water Station 2
Take a break and refill your water at the second water station.
*Coords: 37.7755, -122.42*

### 5. [END] Back Entrance
Complete your loop by returning to the back entrance of KBR Park.
*Coords: 37.7757, -122.4202*
