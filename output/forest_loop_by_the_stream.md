# 🌲 Forest Loop by the Stream

> **Summary:** A peaceful 5km loop through a forest with a streamside walk.

- **Distance:** 5.0 km
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Est. Duration:** 120 mins
- **Elevation Gain:** +50m

---

## 📍 Waypoints

### 1. [START] Start Point
Begin your hike at the forest entrance near the parking lot.
*Coords: 40.7128, -74.006*

### 2. [WATER_STOP] Streamside Walk
Follow the stream for a refreshing walk. Enjoy the sound of water and the cool breeze.
*Coords: 40.713, -74.0058*

### 3. [SCENIC_VIEW] Scenic Viewpoint
Pause here to take in the beautiful view of the forest and the stream.
*Coords: 40.7132, -74.0056*

### 4. [END] End Point
Return to the starting point, completing the loop.
*Coords: 40.7128, -74.006*
